Common questions

How do I cancel Hemli Plus?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Hemli.

I'm not getting reminders.
Open Settings, then Notifications, then Hemli, and make sure notifications are allowed.

Where is my data stored?
On your device only. If you delete the app, your data is deleted with it, unless your device backup keeps a copy.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
In Hemli, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
