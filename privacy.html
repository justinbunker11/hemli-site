Hemli Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Hemli ("the app") is published by Justin Bunker ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

The short version. The app stores your information on your own device. We do not run accounts or servers for your data, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your tasks, reminders, checklists and history.
  • Details you choose to enter, such as appliance model numbers, warranty dates, vehicle information and notes.
  • Photos you choose to add to your home binder or belongings list.

This information stays on your device. We cannot see it. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it, unless your device backup keeps a copy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device. They are created and delivered locally. We do not send your notifications through our own servers.

Photos

The app only accesses photos you pick yourself. It does not read your photo library in the background.

Purchases

Hemli Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple handles billing, renewals and cancellations through your Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

Analytics and tracking

The app does not include advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hemli.support@gmail.com.
